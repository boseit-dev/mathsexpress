## What free accounts can't do (enforced on the server, not just hidden)
| Rule | Where it's enforced |
|---|---|
| 15 questions a day (Sydney time), shared across all practice | `mathsexpress_record_daily_question_attempt` refuses the 16th. The app checks the count before starting practice and before each next question. |
| No AI tutor and no AI step marking | `mathsexpress_consume_ai_quota`. Every `/api/ai` and `/api/mark-step` call goes through it, so editing the page can't get round it. |
| 3 hints a day | `mathsexpress_use_hint` (asking for the same hint again doesn't count) |
| Rewards for at most 15 questions a day | `mathsexpress_award_question_reward` |
| Limited topics (first 2 skills in each strand), no adaptive practice, quizzes and tests, daily challenges, personalised revision or Mistake Book, growth report, or games other than Quick Maths | Blocked in the app before any other click handler runs, with an upgrade window. These features run in the browser, so the server-side guarantee is the question limit and reward cap above. |

- **Plans can't be faked.** Plans are stored in `mathsexpress_entitlements`, which users can't read or write. Only the server (after a verified Stripe payment) or the owner/admin can grant one. A payment can't be applied twice.
- **Primary Pro and Secondary Pro only cover their own year levels.** For example, Primary Pro doesn't unlock a Year 9 student.
- **Teacher-set class work is never blocked by the free limit.** This only applies while the student actually has that assignment open, so the exemption can't be faked.
- **Schools:**
  - Students in a school with an active licence get everything.
  - **Every school that exists today is licensed automatically**, so current classes keep working.
  - The owner can turn a school's licence on or off.
- **Staff accounts** (teacher, admin, owner) are unaffected.

## Payments
- The Worker has `/api/billing/checkout`, which creates a Stripe Checkout session in AUD for a 1-year plan.
- `/api/billing/webhook` checks Stripe's signature, the amount and the currency, then grants the plan for 365 days.
- **This needs 3 Worker secrets:** `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`. Until they're set, "Choose Plan" says "Online payments are not set up yet", and no plan can be bought.
- In Stripe, add a webhook to `https://<your domain>/api/billing/webhook` for the `checkout.session.completed` event.
- The owner console's user detail panel now has **Plan + Days → Set plan**, for manual sales and refunds.

## In the app
- A "N of 15 free questions left today" counter shows for free students.
- An upgrade window recommends the right plan for the student's year.
- The profile shows the current plan and its expiry date.
- After payment, the app checks for the new plan again and shows it once Stripe confirms.

Accessibility Statement — Draft

MathsExpress aims to support a wide range of learners and to work toward WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable.

Features include high contrast, dark mode, larger text, reduced motion, dyslexia-friendly settings, read-aloud support, keyboard controls, labels/live regions and accessible alternatives for several interactive question types.

Before claiming conformance

The production release should complete and document keyboard-only, screen-reader, contrast, zoom/reflow, form error/status, maths notation, interactive graph, media and timed-assessment accommodation testing.

Accessibility contact

Support: sign in to MathsExpress and use Report bug / suggestion, or use the support channel supplied by your school/deployment administrator.

# MathsExpress textbook catalogue — syllabus-aware outline and reader

This file records verified syllabus structure, a richer topic-level reading interface,
and the deliberately limited authored content. It is **not** a claim that all 25 books or outcomes have
been written, mapped, audited or approved.

## What is now authored (31 October 2026)

- The 25 existing textbook covers remain separate: K–10 Mathematics, Years 7–10
  Life Skills, and the distinct Year 11 and 12 Standard, Advanced, Extension and Life Skills courses.
- A typed, original lesson catalogue includes **25 starter lessons**, one in each
  book's first syllabus focus area. The existing detailed Year 9 linear equations
  lesson remains available separately: **26 authored lessons in total**.
- Each starter lesson teaches a concept, offers a concrete visual or number pattern,
  explains a worked example line by line, guides one checkpoint, provides two independent
  questions with hints and detailed feedback, and ends with three revision points.
- Starter lessons are **not** equivalent to finished focus areas or complete syllabus
  coverage. A clear explanation appears on each such lesson. The rest of the
  focus-area outlines remain labelled In preparation.
- NESA sets the K–10 syllabus by stage, not a prescribed chapter order for each
  calendar year. Year-specific examples are original editorial choices. Years 7–10
  Core and Stage 5 Paths remain separate; Years 11–12 have different syllabus courses.
- Life Skills starter lessons are optional examples only. Teachers select suitable
  outcomes and content for individual students.
- Answers in textbook practice are session-only. Saved student scores and teacher
  reports use the separate Learn lesson system, not these new static examples.

## Outstanding before describing any book as syllabus-complete

1. Map every official outcome and content point to its correctly scoped stage and course,
   including Stage 5 Core/Paths and K–6 A/B, with NESA source links and record-of-changes date.
2. Confirm the syllabus version currently applicable to each cohort and the special options
   for Life Skills. The student's teacher-assigned year level must not be changed by browsing.
3. Have original chapter explanations, questions, diagrams, worked solutions and extension
   questions independently reviewed for mathematical correctness, age level and accessibility.
4. Check coverage and avoid falsely claiming a concept is compulsory in a particular year
   when NESA specifies it at stage level.
5. Add question types beyond numeric input, chapter bookmarking, saved responses, teacher
   controls and printing in later tested increments.
6. Run independent educational review before public launch, not just automated type checks.

## Topic outline upgrade — 31 October 2026

The 25 books now list official stage-level and Stage 6 course focus-area headings instead of
three generic placeholders. Each topic is independently addressable and searchable in the
book contents. Only the Year 9 equations pilot is flagged as authored.
The existing Year 9 pilot uses an interactive Study Notebook. All 25 other books now have
an original concept-first starter lesson using the same five-section teaching structure.

**Not yet achieved:** a full official outcome/content-point mapping for every stage,
hundreds of original reviewed explanations, example banks, questions, diagrams, or
teacher-approved K–12 coverage. Heading coverage is not lesson coverage.

## Copyright and accuracy

Create original MathsExpress wording, examples, images, exercises and cover artwork.
Textbook publishers can inspire general features (contents, exercises, worked examples),
but Cambridge and other protected pages, illustrations and questions must not be copied.
Use the official public NSW syllabuses as the curriculum reference, with attribution.

## Additional authored introductions — 31 October 2026

An extra original lesson bank now introduces every _listed catalogue focus-area heading_
across the 25 books, not just each book's first heading. This includes K–6 stage-level
topics, Stage 4/5 Core, Stage 5 Paths and distinct Standard, Advanced, Extension and
Life Skills 11–12 focus areas. Some K–10 and Life Skills content is purposefully shared
across neighbouring books because outcomes are defined at the stage or individual
learning-plan level rather than by a fixed year.

**Critical distinction:** The presence of a teaching introduction does **not** mean the
whole focus area, all NSW outcomes, every content point or full required depth is finished.
Every new topic is a starting example with one worked solution, a guided answer and
two practice questions. Further concepts, richer diagrams, multi-step worked problems,
extension questions, student progress saving and expert review remain outstanding.
See the official course links in each textbook for NESA's complete requirements.

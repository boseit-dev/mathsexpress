# MathsExpress textbook catalogue — initial curriculum record

This file records verified syllabus structure and the deliberately limited content
of the first implementation. It is **not** a claim that all 25 books or outcomes have
been written, mapped, audited or approved.

## What this pilot builds

- Separate, addressable MathsExpress book covers for K through Year 10 and Years 7–10
  Life Skills; separate Year 11 Standard, Advanced, Extension 1 and Life Skills books;
  separate Year 12 Standard 1, Standard 2, Advanced, Extension 1, Extension 2 and Life Skills books.
- Every book has its own contents and an original getting-started page. Book and chapter
  navigation is real and tested. Catalogue search is based on title and stage.
- The broad headings of Number and algebra, Measurement and space, and Statistics and
  probability reflect the K–10 syllabus. These headings are **not** complete yearly
  outcome maps. NESA specifies outcomes by stage and permits teacher programming choices;
  do not imply a textbook year sequence is prescribed by the syllabus.
- Standard 11 and Standard 1/2 Year 12 have their distinct focus-area titles from NESA.
  Content is not yet published for those titles.
- One original pilot chapter: Year 9, solving linear equations. Includes a worked example,
  six numerical questions, hints, retry feedback and simple answer checking. Equations
  and answers are verified by unit tests. No user answers or scores are persisted.
- The library can filter by assigned year and by books with published maths chapters.
  It reports actual availability: only the Year 9 equations pilot has maths lessons at
  this stage. The introduction guide is not counted as a complete maths chapter.
- Readers can move between adjacent chapters and (if teacher-assigned Year 9) navigate
  between the textbook pilot and the published guided Learn lesson. All answers remain
  session-only, with no account progress saving.

## Outstanding before describing any book as syllabus-complete

1. Map every official outcome and content point to its correctly scoped stage and course,
   including Stage 5 Core/Paths and K–6 A/B, with NESA source links and record-of-changes date.
2. Confirm the syllabus version currently applicable to each cohort and the special options
   for Life Skills. The student's teacher-assigned year level must not be changed by browsing.
3. Have original chapter explanations, questions, diagrams, worked solutions and extension
   questions independently reviewed for mathematical correctness, age level and accessibility.
4. Check coverage and avoid falsely claiming a concept is compulsory in a particular year
   when NESA specifies it at stage level.
5. Add question types beyond numeric input, chapter bookmarking, saved responses, teacher
   controls and printing in later tested increments.
6. Run independent educational review before public launch, not just automated type checks.

## Copyright and accuracy

Create original MathsExpress wording, examples, images, exercises and cover artwork.
Textbook publishers can inspire general features (contents, exercises, worked examples),
but Cambridge and other protected pages, illustrations and questions must not be copied.
Use the official public NSW syllabuses as the curriculum reference, with attribution.
